Section 02

Assignment obligations by underlying

Review short option contracts, possible cash purchases and share delivery, recorded opening premium, and positions with an estimated chance above 60% of finishing in the money.

Preparing your open option book…

Premium is the reconciled opening cash flow for still-open short options, before fees. It is not realized profit. High risk counts open option rows, not contracts; missing matching quotes are marked unscored. Assignment figures are gross obligations and do not net protective long options or premium received.

    Money columns use the reporting currency. Average cost is total cost ÷ shares; Last is market value ÷ shares after currency conversion. Different native prices are combined by share quantity.
