Data coverage & how to read this page

    Gross put purchase obligation = strike × contract multiplier × short contracts for physical puts. It assumes all recorded puts are assigned, excludes hedge offsets, and is neither maximum loss nor required margin. Short calls and cash-settled options are not included in that figure.

    Concentration uses the share of gross put purchase obligations. It does not measure total portfolio exposure. Snapshots over four calendar days old are marked out of date; no current structural assessment is made from them.
