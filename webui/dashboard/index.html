Expand a stock to see its individual contracts and roll arrows. Select a contract to highlight its roll chain; press Escape to clear the selection. A dashed lead-in before the first recorded point means earlier history is unavailable. Solid bars show recorded opening-to-close trades or position observations; dashed extensions show time remaining to expiry and do not confirm a position is still open. Dots show recorded opens and closes. Roll arrows retain the broker evidence level; possible links are dashed.
