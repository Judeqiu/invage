What this index measures

The same fixed grid is used for every contract: underlying moves of −30%, −15%, 0%, +15%, +30%, combined with IV shifts of −20, 0, +20 percentage points (floored at 1% IV), after five calendar days or at expiry if sooner. These are test scenarios, not forecast confidence ranges.

Reported option last-trade price, underlying regular-session price, IV and quote timestamps come from the exact Yahoo Finance contract. Dividend yield is a continuous proxy from reported trailing-year distributions. Ambiguous post-split distribution bases are rejected. The interest rate is the latest available FRED DGS1 observation, converted to continuous compounding as a flat one-year proxy. Estimated exit costs come from Management.

American option values use a 200-step Jarrow–Rudd tree. Discrete dividends, adjusted contracts, portfolio offsets, stressed liquidity and broker margin are outside scope. Prices and volatility can change between observations. Expiry loss bounds exclude stock positions created by exercise or assignment.

Expected Shortfall and account equity impact are unavailable. This contract-level index does not use account equity. No calibrated probabilities or universal risk grades are implied. A finite stress grid cannot represent an uncovered call’s unbounded loss.
