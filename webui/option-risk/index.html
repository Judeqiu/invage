How this index is calculated

Index = 100 × max(0, worst grid loss) / account equity. The grid combines five underlying moves (−shock, −half shock, unchanged, +half shock, +shock) with three IV shifts (−shock, unchanged, +shock). Price and IV shocks occur jointly at the horizon.

European contracts use Black–Scholes with continuous dividend yield. American contracts use a 200-step Jarrow–Rudd binomial approximation with early exercise; this is an estimate, not the connected portfolio QuantLib engine. Discrete dividends, adjusted deliverables, futures options, exotic payoffs and multi-leg hedges are outside scope. Exercise or assignment can create stock positions whose subsequent risk is outside the option payoff.

The current entered premium is compared directly with each theoretical scenario price. A quote/model mismatch can materially affect results. Prices are manual and not verified live. Costs are a fixed user estimate, not a liquidity model. Portfolio offsets, margin calls, volatility skew dynamics, extreme moves outside the grid and parameter uncertainty are not captured.

Expected Shortfall and probability of profit are unavailable: this grid has no calibrated real-world probabilities. A zero score only means none of these scenarios produced a loss. No universal low/medium/high thresholds have been validated.

Method references: CME scenario risk · OCC option sensitivities · FINRA assignment risks. This simplified index is not an exchange margin model.
