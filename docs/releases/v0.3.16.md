# Invage v0.3.16

Released 2026-10-12 from `main`. Utarus remains pinned to `v4.2.8`.

## Changes

- The option timeline groups contracts by underlying stock and starts each stock collapsed into a summary row with contract count, account count, activity markers and expiry range. The summary range does not establish a continuous holding.
- Clicking a stock, or pressing Enter or Space on its header, expands its separate contract rows and recorded roll arrows. Expand all and Collapse all operate on the filtered stocks. Expansion choices survive filtering and refreshes; collapsing preserves the date scale.
- Recorded opens and closes use distinct marker offsets in the summary. Broker and account evidence stays separate in the expanded view. Mobile horizontal scrolling and keyboard evidence details remain available.
- Includes the previously deployed dashed lead-in when opening history is missing. It marks an evidence boundary without estimating an opening date or holding period.
