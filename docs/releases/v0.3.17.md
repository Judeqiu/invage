# Invage v0.3.17

Released 2026-10-12 from `main`. Utarus remains pinned to `v4.2.8`.

## Changes

- Tiger position imports extract the verified stock symbol from full option codes before building holding keys. All broker syncs and custom statement imports enforce this normalization before books writes.
- Execution and lifecycle imports normalize stock symbols while retaining broker IDs and monetary evidence. Codes that contradict explicit expiry, strike or put/call fields fail validation; duplicate normalized positions are rejected.
- Retained option observations normalize during the next successful account sync. History reads and retained relationship archives use the same symbols, preserving observation dates and contract continuity across the change.
- Different contracts remain distinct by strike, expiry, side and account, while the option timeline collapses them under their stock.
