# Invage v0.3.19

Released 2026-10-12 from `main`. Utarus remains pinned to `v4.2.8`.

## Changes

- The option timeline fills the available frame width and redraws its date axis, bars and stock rows when the container changes size, without fetching history again. Narrow screens retain a readable minimum width with horizontal scrolling.
- Hovering a contract row or a fill marker shows dated recorded prices: entry cost, position mark, quantity, execution sell/buy price, gross premium and commission where available. Unit and contract prices are labeled separately; unavailable prices remain explicit. These are recorded prices, not live bid/ask quotes.
- Collapsed stock markers and keyboard focus also show price cards. Cards stay within the viewport and dismiss on Escape, scrolling, filtering or layout changes. Replay cards exclude future observations and fills.
