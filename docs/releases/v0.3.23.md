# Velovest v0.3.23

Source release dated 2026-11-22 from `main`. Utarus is pinned to `v4.2.52`
(`ed33fb199f6db79c9ea448f2c8040174f6ad0755`).

## Options decision reports

- Ask AI Chat to generate your options decision report now, or choose
  **Questions → Reports → Generate options decision report** and send the
  prepared message. Each new request scans with the data available at that
  time, including outside trading hours. The chat reply links to the full
  report in your private Notes.
- To receive the daily scan, opt in under **Settings → Premarket options**.
  It is off by default. The daily scan is scheduled for 8 p.m. Singapore time on
  U.S. trading days while the market is still in premarket. Daily notifications
  are sent when positions qualify or evidence is incomplete. On-demand requests
  run independently of the setting and the daily schedule.
- The scan covers standalone short American puts expiring from the U.S. scan
  date through 28 calendar days later, inclusive, with underlying price at or
  below 110% of strike. The report compares holding through possible
  assignment, selling a covered call after assignment, rolling the put, and
  closing it. It gives quote timestamps and marks stale or missing evidence,
  and distinguishes unavailable coverage from a scan with no matches.
- Reports are saved and verified in Notes before notification. Notifications
  contain only a title and the Notes link. The scan submits no orders.

## Other changes since v0.3.22

- Added personal Account Risk and dashboard day-to-day settings. Dashboard
  and trade views now show more consistent valuation, exposure, premium,
  roll-date and realized P&L evidence, including matched opening fills for
  recorded closing trades and updated handling of protective option legs.
- Expanded broker sync and reconciliation for current snapshots, historical
  fills, fees, debit cash and incomplete imports. Added queryable broker
  source archives, account-scoped cash ledgers, MooMoo and Futu fund evidence,
  and saved account asset-value queries with FX and coverage details.
  Historical cash observations remain separate from posted balances.
- Added the Velovest homepage and signup workflow, including private
  payment-proof review, queued access emails, manager-controlled signup dates
  and renewal without an onboarding appointment. Added weekly AI credit
  display and active manager exemptions.

## Validation

The release build and 72 focused regression tests passed. Version metadata
matches the package lock, with the existing dependency pins retained.

The options-report implementation was exercised through Chromium using
disposable accounts and storage. The journey covered free-text chat submission
and the prepared preset, manual weekend and premarket runs with the schedule
disabled, note and notification links, owner isolation, and an independent
scheduled run. The test used broker and model response fixtures. Live model
interpretation of free-text input was outside its coverage.

## Rollback

Use the previous verified deployment artifact for the affected service. Preserve
the database, private Notes and retained broker evidence. The options-report
change requires no schema downgrade.
